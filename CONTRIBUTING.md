# Contributing to Hearthside Source Edition

Thank you for wanting to improve Hearthside.

## Before opening a contribution

- Use synthetic data. Do not include private conversations, account exports,
  bot tokens, API keys, credentials, or identifying screenshots.
- Keep changes focused and explain how you tested them.
- Preserve file headers, notices, provenance records, and third-party licences.
- Do not add release credentials, official updater access, or R2 publishing
  paths to the source edition.

## Rights and contribution terms

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to submit
it. You license the contribution under the same terms that govern the part of
Hearthside it modifies:

- contributor-owned additions expressly identified as Original Hearthside
  Material are provided under Apache-2.0 with the “Commons Clause” License
  Condition v1.0 and the additional permission in LICENSE; and
- contributions to separately licensed third-party or upstream material must
  comply with that material's existing licence and must not remove its notices.

You retain copyright in your contribution. Submission does not transfer
ownership to the maintainers.

Changes to upstream-derived files do not alter the licence of upstream
material. Clearly identify whether your new copyrightable additions are being
submitted as Original Hearthside Material under the Hearthside terms or solely
under the upstream project's licence.

Add this sign-off to commits to certify the statement above:

```text
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email.example>
```

## Security and privacy

Do not publish a suspected vulnerability containing live credentials or
private user data in an issue. Contact the maintainers privately before sharing
sensitive details. A dedicated security-reporting route will be documented
before the repository becomes public.
